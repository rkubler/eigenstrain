Residual stresses · compatibility · eigenstrain

How an incompatible strain creates residual stress

An eigenstrain ε* is a strain that is not caused by stress: plastic flow, thermal expansion, a phase change, an interference fit. When ε* is incompatible, the parts no longer fit together. Forcing them back into one continuous body requires elastic strains, and those elastic strains carry self-equilibrated residual stresses.
